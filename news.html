Nexis News

Nexis News is the public record for development updates, shard notices, feature changes and important world information. It gives players and visitors one place to see what has changed without requiring an account or opening a private game screen.

Nexis is a persistent fantasy browser RPG where character progression, education, travel, trade, organisations, property and reputation contribute to a long-running world. Public dispatches document meaningful changes to those systems and highlight updates that affect how players interact with the live experience.

Follow the public world

Use the news board for current notices, then explore the rules, staff, contact and credits pages for the stable public information around the game. Private player data and account-only application routes remain outside the public search surface.
