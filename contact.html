Contact Nexis

The Nexis contact page provides the official public routes for player support, rule and abuse reports, bug reporting and project communication. Visitors can find the appropriate channel without needing a game account or exposing private account information to a public page.

For rule violations or suspected abuse, include enough factual detail for staff to investigate without posting sensitive information publicly. For technical issues, explain what happened, what you expected to happen and the relevant part of the game so the problem can be reproduced rather than guessed at.

Support without exposing private systems

Public contact information is intentionally separated from account controls, administrative tools and private player data. That boundary lets search engines understand how to reach the project while keeping authenticated game surfaces outside the public index.
