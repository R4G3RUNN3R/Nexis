Nexis Credits

The Nexis credits page records the project identity, publishing attribution and public acknowledgements behind the persistent fantasy browser RPG. It gives contributors, players and search engines a stable place to understand who develops and maintains the experience as the project grows.

Nexis is developed and published by Voidsmith Industries. The game combines character progression, education, travel, trade, organisations, property, reputation and persistent-world systems inside a browser-based command interface designed for repeatable long-term play.

Project attribution

Credits can expand as additional contributors, services and licensed resources become part of the project. Keeping this information on a dedicated public page preserves clear ownership and attribution without exposing internal development systems, private repositories or account-only application data.
