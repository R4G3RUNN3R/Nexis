Voidsmith Industries presents

Nexis

Nexis is a persistent fantasy browser RPG where progression comes from what your character learns, owns, survives and builds over time. Enter a living world shaped by education, travel, organisations, trade, reputation and long-term character choices.

Character progression

Develop your character through education, skills, equipment, property, work and reputation rather than a single linear level path.

A persistent world

Travel between places, interact with institutions and organisations, and return to a world that keeps track of your choices and progress.

Trade and ownership

Use markets, inventory, property and organisation systems to build a practical place in the economy instead of collecting disconnected rewards.

Reputation and organisations

Guilds, consortiums and reputation systems give social and organisational choices lasting weight. Your place in the world is shaped by more than what you carry or how hard you can hit.

A fantasy world built for the browser

Nexis brings its systems together in a command-style interface designed for direct, repeatable play. Character management, travel, learning, trade and world activity live inside one persistent experience, letting short visits contribute to longer goals. The project is developed by Voidsmith Industries as an evolving browser RPG, with the public homepage kept separate from private game routes so search engines can understand the game without indexing personal or application-only screens.
