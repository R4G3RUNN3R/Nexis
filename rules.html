Nexis Rules

Nexis is designed around long-term progression in a persistent world, so fair play matters more than a single match or short session. The public rules protect player accounts, progression, the economy, social spaces and the integrity of systems that are intended to retain consequences over time.

Exploits, automation that bypasses intended play, abuse of other players, account manipulation and attempts to gain unfair progression advantages can damage a persistent environment far beyond one interaction. The live rules page is the authoritative player-facing reference for acceptable behaviour and enforcement expectations.

Protecting a persistent world

Players should review the current rules before relying on edge cases or unusual mechanics. Bugs and suspected abuse should be reported rather than exploited. Staff can investigate reports while legitimate players continue using normal game systems without having private account information exposed to search engines.
